Teaser (phase 5)

Open ?fast — delay 2s, interval 4s instead of 8s/45s.

  1. Bubble appears after ~2s, above the pill, never covering the FAB. Solid white, 1px border, tail pointing down-right.
  2. Three messages rotate in order: IELTS/PTE fees → IELTS vs PTE → batch timings. After the third it stops permanently.
  3. Each appearance auto-hides after 7s if ignored.
  4. Dismiss with × → never returns. Reload the page → still gone (fourskills.teaser.done survives navigation).
  5. Open the chat instead of dismissing → also never returns.
  6. Click the bubble text → opens the chat.
  7. Reload mid-rotation → the count carries over, you do not get three fresh bubbles on every page.
  8. Turn on OS "reduce motion" → bubble appears with no scale/fade, same timings.

Open ?resume — seeds a stored conversation.

  1. Gold unread dot on the FAB immediately, panel still closed.
  2. One bubble reading "Continue your chat". No rotation, no repeats.
  3. Opening the chat clears both, and the stored thread is there with the greeting shown once and no starter chips.

Lead form (phase 6)

Click Request a call back in the panel footer, or let the bot offer it.

  1. Never blocks the chat: with the form open, the composer below is still live. Type and send a message — it should work, and the form should stay put.
  2. Course dropdown is pre-selected from what you asked about earlier in the thread.
  3. Bad phone (0412345) → inline error under the field, red border, nothing sent.
  4. Valid: 0332 241 0155, +92 332 241 0155, 00923322410155.
  5. Submit → confirmation in the thread and a green "Continue on WhatsApp" button whose link is prefilled with the name and course.
  6. Decline: dismiss with ×, then keep chatting. The bot must never re-offer the form for the rest of the session, however interested you sound. The footer "Request a call back" link comes back, because that is the visitor's choice, not ours.
  7. After a successful submit the footer link disappears and stays gone across reloads.
